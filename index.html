How to Play

Each piece is a live slice of the same looping video. Reassemble them into the complete moving picture on the faint dashed outline.

  • Drag a piece to move it. Pieces that are already joined move together as one group.
  • Tap a piece (a quick press without dragging) to rotate it 90°. Pieces start at random angles, so you'll need to match the orientation as well as the position.
  • Connect two pieces by dragging one next to its correct neighbor at the matching angle — they snap together with a click and from then on move and rotate as a single group.
  • Solve it by fitting every piece onto the dashed outline. When the whole picture is assembled it locks into place and celebrates.

Toolbar

  • 15 / 24 / 35 / 48 pcs — choose how many pieces. More pieces means a harder puzzle; switching restarts the puzzle.
  • Hint — then tap any piece to reveal where it belongs on the board.
  • Reshuffle — scatter all the pieces and start the current puzzle over.

Your progress is saved automatically — you can leave a puzzle and come back to it right where you left off.
